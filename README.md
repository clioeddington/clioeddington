# Clio Eddington

I am a publishing professional based in Norwich. I edit business blogs, coordinate releases and help small teams make their editorial work easier to follow.

My work connects structure with practical delivery: clear briefs, thoughtful revisions, useful handover notes and realistic publishing schedules. I enjoy turning a collection of drafts into something a reader can use comfortably.

I am interested in simple digital tools that reduce avoidable admin and leave more time for careful editing. I value clear documentation and a visible owner for the next step.

Away from the screen, I enjoy bookbinding, independent print workshops and museum visits. Those interests keep me thinking about how information feels on a page, as well as what it says.
